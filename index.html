Our Company

Quality is our commitment. Innovation is our step.

PILARK Composites is the composite solutions brand of PT. Panca Integra Laguna Reksa. The 2026 Company Profile presents PILAR as an Indonesian FRP solutions provider for industrial, residential and commercial applications.

The profile describes group manufacturing experience beginning in 1983 and capabilities including hand lay-up, cold press, hot press, molded grating, vacuum infusion, 3D printing, and roofing & cladding systems.
